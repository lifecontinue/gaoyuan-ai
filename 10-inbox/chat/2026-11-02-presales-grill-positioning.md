来源：Claude Code 对话，2026-11-02，PM 本人口述。只收不改；判断见 `20-intake/2026-11/presales-platform/plan.md` §0.5。

做到现在，我感觉很困惑，到底在做一件什么样的事情？

我这个产品的定位是面向我们公司的售前人员，需要他们了解一些客户的基本信息，根据客户的信息判断需要做哪些相应信息的补充，或者给到对应的解答，逐渐让他们明确出来，类似于一个grill me skill。

Based on user requirements, analyze what can be implemented right now or how customer needs can be met. Review them one by one, and for anything that genuinely cannot be met, list it separately so they can quickly copy and send it to the product team.

Once all requirements are finalized, generate a user story or scenario in HTML format. This makes it easy for them to report back and communicate with the customer.
